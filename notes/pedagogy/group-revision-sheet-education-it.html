⭐ HIGH-YIELD EXAM FACTS

A. Micro-Teaching (नया — अत्यंत महत्वपूर्ण)

  • उत्पत्ति: Stanford University, USA, 1963; प्रवर्तक — Dwight Allen (Bush, Allen, McDonald, Acheson टीम); Ford व Kettering Foundation द्वारा वित्तपोषित।
  • भारत में शुरुआत: 1967, D.D. Tiwari, Govt. Central Pedagogical Institute, Allahabad। इसके बाद: G.B. Shaw (1970, M.S. University Baroda), N.L. Dosajh (Chandigarh)।
  • परिभाषा (Allen, 1966): "Scaled down teaching encounter in class size and class-time"
  • विशेषताएँ: 5-10 छात्र, 5-10 मिनट, एक समय में एक ही skill, तुरंत feedback, controlled situation।
  • Cycle (6 चरण): Plan → Teach → Feedback → Re-plan → Re-teach → Re-feedback
  • Indian Model (NCERT): 5-10 छात्र, Teaching 6 मिनट, कुल Cycle 36 मिनट (Teach 6+Feedback 6+Replan 12+Reteach 6+Refeedback 6)
  • 3 Phases: (1) Knowledge Acquisition (Pre-active) (2) Skill Acquisition (Inter-active) (3) Transfer Phase (Post-active)

B. Teaching Skills (Micro-Teaching के अंतर्गत)

  • Reinforcement — 4+1 घटक: PVR (Positive Verbal), PNVR (Positive Non-Verbal), NVR (Negative Verbal), NNVR (Negative Non-Verbal) + WPAB (Writing Pupil's Answer on Black Board)
  • Explaining — 4 घटक: CL (Cognitive Link), ILL (Illustrations), CC (Comparing & Contrasting), MR (Meaningful Repetition)
  • Stimulus Variation — 6 घटक: Teacher Movement, Teacher Gesture, Change in Speech Pattern, Change in Sensory Focus, Pupil Talk, Pupil Movement
  • Questioning — घटक: Structure, Variety (Low/High order), Pause, Voice, Reinforcement, Fluency, Distribution
  • Probing Questioning — 5 techniques: Prompting, Seeking Further Information, Refocussing, Re-direction, Increasing Critical Awareness
  • Using Blackboard — 8 घटक: Legibility, Size & Alignment, Highlighting Main Points, Utilization of Space, Correctness, Position of Teacher, Eye Contact with Pupils, Clearing of Blackboard
  • Demonstration — 5 घटक: Appropriate topic (A), Sequence Order of Presentation (SOP), Adequacy of Manipulative Skill (AMS), Creation of Appropriate Situation (CAS), Generalization (G)
  • Evaluation Tool: S.T.A.G. (Stanford Teacher Competence Appraisal Guide) — Allen & Ryan (1969)
  • Allen & Ryans (1969) — 14 Teaching Skills सूची; B.K. Passi (1979) — पुस्तक "Becoming a Better Teacher" में 13 skills

C. Link Practice

  • परिभाषा: एक साथ 3-4 skills को integrate करके teach करना, Micro-teaching व वास्तविक class के बीच सेतु।
  • छात्र संख्या: 15-20, Duration: 20 मिनट, Skills: 3-4
  • दो प्रकार: Integration in Parts और Integration as a whole

D. Lesson Planning

  • Year/Annual Plan: शैक्षिक वर्ष के आरंभ में संपूर्ण course हेतु; Units, Periods, Working days, Holidays शामिल।
  • Unit Plan: Year Plan व Lesson Plan के बीच का माध्यम; 2 प्रकार — Resource Unit व Teaching Unit
  • Herbartian Lesson Plan — 5 चरण (आधार: Apperceptive Mass Theory): Preparation → Presentation → Comparison & Abstraction → Generalization → Application
  • Lesson Plan Outline (10 बिंदु): Subject/Class/Topic → General Objective → Specific Objective → Introduction → Objective Statement → Elucidation → Development Question → Black-board Summary → Recapitulation Question → Home-work

E. Instructional Objectives — Bloom's Action Verbs

  • Cognitive: Knowledge→Recall/Define/State; Comprehension→Explain/Classify; Application→Compute/Demonstrate; Analysis→Divide/Compare; Synthesis→Argue/Summarise; Evaluation→Decide/Criticize
  • Affective: Receiving→Responding→Valuing→Conceptualization→Organization→Characterization
  • Psychomotor: Perception→Set→Guided Response→Mechanism→Complex Overt Response
  • 🔗 पहले cover किया गया Bloom Taxonomy (1956) यहाँ Action-Verb स्तर तक विस्तृत हुआ

G. Individualized Instruction (IT ↔ Pedagogy Cross-Link 🔗)

  • Programmed Learning — आधार: B.F. Skinner का Operant Conditioning सिद्धांत
  • 5 Principles: Small Steps, Active Responding, Immediate Confirmation, Self Pacing, Evaluation
  • 3 Types: Linear (B.F. Skinner) — सीधी क्रम-रेखा; Branched (Norman Crowder) — गलत उत्तर पर Remedial Frame branch (Backward/Forward Branching); Mathetics (Thomas F. Gilbert)
वर्षघटना (CAI Timeline)
1925Pressey's Multiple-choice Machine (Sydney Pressey, Ohio State Univ.) — प्रथम CAI उपकरण
1950Norman Crowder — U.S. Air Force हेतु Branching Programme
1954B.F. Skinner — Teaching Machine, University of Pittsburgh
1956SAKI (Self-Adaptive Keyboard Instructor) — Gordon Pask & Robin McKinnon-Wood
1959PLATO Project शुरू — University of Illinois
1959IBM का "Coursewriter" प्रणाली विकास आरंभ
  • CAI के 5 Modes: Drill & Practice, Tutorial Mode, Simulation Mode, Discovery Mode, Gaming Mode
  • CAI के 4 Steps: Analysis → Design → Programming → Validation Phase
  • CMI: Computer स्वयं नहीं पढ़ाता, बल्कि objectives/resources/assessment manage करता है

⚠ Frequently Confused Facts

  • Micro-Teaching उत्पत्ति वर्ष (Stanford 1963) ≠ भारत में शुरुआत (1967)
  • Analytic (Unknown→Known) ≠ Synthetic (Known→Unknown) — उल्टा याद होने का खतरा
  • Skinner = Linear Programming; Skinner ≠ Branched (वह Crowder है)
  • CAI ≠ CMI (एक सिखाता है, दूसरा management करता है)
