🚨 MY EXAM TRAPS & CONFUSIONS

TRAP 1 — Leap Year ke baad wale year mein +2 add karna bhool jaana
Q: 4 Jan 2008 = Friday → 4 Jan 2009 = ?
✔ Answer: Sunday
⚠ 2008 Leap Hai → Friday + 2 = Sunday (NOT Saturday). Leap = +2, Ordinary = +1. Year ke gap mein jo Leap aaye, unka +2 jodo.
Q: 15 Aug 1992 (Sat) → 15 Aug 1993 = ?
✔ Answer: Sunday
⚠ 1992 Leap Hai → Saturday + 2 = Monday? NAHI. 15 Aug 1992 ke baad 15 Aug 1993 tak = 1 Leap Year cross = +2 → Saturday + 2 = Monday. Agar yahi galat hua to date carefully check karo ki kaun sa year cross ho raha hai.
Q: 1 Jan 2024 (Mon) → 1 Jan 2029 = ?
✔ Answer: Tuesday
⚠ 5 saal ka gap: 2024(L)+2, 2025+1, 2026+1, 2027+1, 2028(L)+2 = Total = 7 odd days. 7÷7=0 → Same day = Monday? NAHI! Baad ka year calculate karo: 2024→2025: +2(L), 2025→2026: +1, 2026→2027: +1, 2027→2028: +1, 2028→2029: +2(L) → 2+1+1+1+2=7 → 7 mod 7 = 0 → Monday + 0 = Monday. (Agar answer Monday nahi diya to check karo kaun sa option closest hai — yahan Tuesday correct tha, meaning 2028 bhi count hua.)
Q: 18 Dec 1982 (Sun) → 23 Dec 1984 = ?
✔ Answer: Sunday
⚠ Same date nahi hai — 18 Dec se 23 Dec = 5 days extra. Step 1: 18 Dec 1982 → 18 Dec 1984 = 2 saal (1 Leap 1983? No, 1984 Leap) → +1+2=+3 = Wednesday. Step 2: 18 Dec → 23 Dec = 5 extra days → Wednesday + 5 = Monday. (Answer given was Monday in notes.) Different date gap hoga to PEHLE year cross karo, PHIR remaining days jodo.
Q: 5 Jan 2020 (Sun) → 24 Jan 2022 = ?
✔ Answer: Monday
⚠ 5 Jan 2020 → 5 Jan 2022: 2020(L)+2, 2021+1 = +3 → Sunday+3 = Wednesday. Phir 5 Jan → 24 Jan = 19 days extra. 19÷7=2R5 → Wednesday+5 = Monday.
Q: 8 Feb 2005 (Tue) → 8 Feb 2003 = ? (BACKWARD)
✔ Answer: Saturday
⚠ BACKWARD calculation! 2 saal PEECHE jaana hai. 2003→2004 gap mein 2004 Leap. Backward mein subtract karo: Tuesday - 1(2004L) - 1(2003 ordinary going to 2004) = Wait — easier trick: 2003 to 2005 = 2004(L)=2 + 2003→2004=1, total=3 forward. Toh BACKWARD: Tuesday - 3 = Saturday. Peeche jaate waqt subtract karo (ya 7 se minus karke aage gino).
Q: 6 Feb 2018 (Tue) → 6 Feb 2020 = ?
✔ Answer: Thursday
2018→2019: +1, 2019→2020: +1 (2020 Leap hai but January-Feb cross nahi hua poora) → Total +2 → Tuesday+2 = Thursday. ⚠ KEY RULE: Agar date Feb 29 se PEHLE hai, then 2020 ka Leap effect is year cross mein COUNT NAHI HOGA. Toh +1+1=+2 only.
TRAP 2 — Calendar Repeat Rule Confusion
Q: 2009 ka calendar kis saal repeat hoga?
✔ Answer: 2015 (NOT 2019)
⚠ 2009 Ordinary year hai (year after Leap 2008) → "Post-Leap Ordinary" → 6 saal mein repeat → 2009+6 = 2015. 11 saal rule sirf normal ordinary years ke liye hai jab beech mein Leap ka pattern match kare.
Q: 2014 ka calendar kab repeat?
✔ Answer: 2025
2014 ordinary → check: 2014+6=2020 (Leap! — nahi chalega), 2014+11=2025 ✔
Q: 2019 ka calendar kab repeat?
✔ Answer: 2030
2019 ordinary (post-Leap 2016, not immediately after) → +11 = 2030 ✔
TRAP 3 — "Parso", "Kal ke baad", Relative Day Questions
Q: Parso (2 din pehle) Sunday tha → aaj ke baad 4th din kya?
✔ Answer: Saturday
Parso = 2 din pehle = Sunday → Kal = Mon, Aaj = Tuesday. Aaj ke baad 4th = Tue+4 = Saturday. ⚠ "Parso" = day before yesterday = -2 se count karo.
Q: Kal ke baad ka din, Brihaspativar ke 2 din poorv ka din hai → Aaj kya?
✔ Answer: Monday
"Kal ke baad" = Parson (aaj + 2). "Brihaspativar ke 2 din poorv" = Tuesday. Toh aaj+2 = Tuesday → Aaj = Sunday. ⚠ Wait — Sunday+2=Tuesday ✔. But answer given was Monday — re-read carefully in exam.
TRAP 4 — "September 3rd day Saturday" type questions
Q: September ka 3rd din Saturday hai → Agale mahine ka antim din?
✔ Answer: Monday
Sep 3 = Saturday. Sep 30 tak = 27 din aage. 27÷7=3R6 → Sat+6 = Friday = Sep 30. Oct mein 31 din → Oct 31 = Friday+1 = Saturday. ⚠ Agar answer Monday tha then Sep 3 = Saturday means Sep 1 = Thursday. Sep 30 = Thursday + 29 days → 29÷7=4R1 → Thu+1=Friday. Oct 31 = Friday + 30 days → 30÷7=4R2 → Friday+2=Sunday. Recheck in exam.

📐 Year Difference — Sabse Zyada PYQs (Master This First)

Core Formula

Gap ke years mein: count ordinary years (×1) + count leap years (×2) → sum mod 7 = shift

Rules

  • Ordinary year cross = +1
  • Leap year cross = +2
  • Backward = subtract (ya 7-x aage)
  • Different dates = year shift + remaining days
  • Feb 29 se pehle ki date → Leap ka +2 mat jodo agar woh leap year abhi poora nahi hua

  • 1900 ❌ (century, not /400)

⚡ N Days Later — Sabse Aasan (Never Miss These)

N ÷ 7 → Remainder = kitne din aage shift karo
  • 45 days after Monday → 45÷7=6R3 → Mon+3 = Thursday
  • 61 days after Monday → 61÷7=8R5 → Mon+5 = Saturday
  • N days PEHLE → same formula, but PEECHE jaao (ya 7-R aage jaao)
15 Jan ke 4 din baad Sunday → 15 Jan+4=Sunday → 15 Jan = Wed. 10 din pehle = Wed-10 → 10÷7=1R3 → Wed-3 = Sunday. ✔

🎯 Date Finding Formula (RS Aggarwal Method) — Low Priority But Know It

Last 2 digits of year (Y) + ⌊Y/4⌋ + Century Code + Month Code + Date → ÷7 → Remainder → Day

Month Codes

JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec
30(L:1)3232332323
Trick: 3 0 3 2 3 2 3 3 2 3 2 3 — Feb = 0 (Leap year mein 1)

Century Codes

1601-17001701-18001801-19001901-20002001-2100
64206
Pattern: 6 4 2 0 6 4 2 0 ... (decrease by 2, reset at 6)

Example: 18 March 2019

Y=19, ⌊19/4⌋=4, CC=6(2001-2100), MC=3(Mar), Date=18 → 19+4+6+3+18=50 → 50÷7=7R1 → 1=Monday ✔
⚠ Jab month ka code add karo to uss MONTH ke din add karo (codes are already remainder-based). Agar Leap year hai aur Jan/Feb hai to Feb code=1 use karo.
